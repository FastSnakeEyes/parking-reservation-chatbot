# Parking Rules

- Maximum speed inside all car parks is 10 km/h.
- Park only within marked bays and do not block driveways, ramps or emergency exits.
- Vehicles running on LPG are not allowed in the underground Old Town car park.
- Respect the height limit shown at each entrance. Height limits are listed in each car park's description.
- Reserved spaces are marked with a blue "R" sign and are only for vehicles with an approved reservation.
- EV charging bays are for electric vehicles that are actively charging. Vehicles blocking chargers can be charged a penalty of 20 EUR.
- Car washing, repairs, camping and sleeping in vehicles are not allowed.
- Lost or damaged entry ticket (drive-in customers): the daily maximum rate for each started day is charged.
- VilniusPark is not responsible for items left in vehicles. All car parks have 24/7 CCTV.
- Vehicles left for more than 30 days without a valid reservation may be towed at the owner's cost.
