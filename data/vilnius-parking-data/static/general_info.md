# VilniusPark – General Information

VilniusPark (UAB "VilniusPark") is a parking operator in Vilnius, Lithuania. It runs five car parks across the city: in the Old Town, at the railway and bus station, in the Konstitucijos avenue business district, at Vilnius International Airport, and in Užupis. Drivers can reserve a space in advance through the VilniusPark chat assistant. They can also drive in without a reservation while non-reserved spaces are free.

## Our car parks
- **VilniusPark Old Town**: underground car park near Cathedral Square and Gedimino avenue.
- **VilniusPark Station**: open-air car park next to the railway and bus stations.
- **VilniusPark Business Center**: multi-storey car park in the Šnipiškės business district on the right bank of the Neris.
- **VilniusPark Airport Long-Stay**: open-air long-stay car park a short walk from the airport terminal.
- **VilniusPark Užupis**: small courtyard car park in the Užupis district.

- Emergency line at each car park: intercom at every barrier and payment machine, available 24/7

## Payment
Parking can be paid by bank card (Visa, Mastercard), Apple Pay or Google Pay at the payment machines, at the exit barrier, or in advance for reserved stays. Cash is accepted only at the Station and Airport car parks. Prices are set per car park and may differ on weekdays and weekends. The chat assistant always gives current prices on request.

## Languages
Support is available in Lithuanian, English and Russian.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a reservation?**
No. Reservations guarantee a space, but every car park also has non-reserved spaces for drive-in customers, first come, first served.

**How is the parking fee calculated?**
The fee is charged per started hour up to a daily maximum. Current rates for each car park are available from the chat assistant.

**Can I park a motorcycle?**
Yes. Motorcycles pay the same rate as cars and use marked motorcycle bays where available.

**Are there electric vehicle chargers?**
Yes, at the Old Town, Business Center and Airport car parks. Charging is paid separately at the charger.

**Is there parking for people with disabilities?**
All car parks have marked accessible spaces near the exits or elevators. Holders of a valid disability parking card park free of charge for up to 2 hours at the Old Town and Užupis car parks.

**Where can I find opening hours?**
Opening hours differ between car parks and can change on public holidays. Ask the chat assistant for the current opening hours.
