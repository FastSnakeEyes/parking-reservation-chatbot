# VilniusPark Old Town (Underground)

## Overview
Underground car park at the edge of Vilnius Old Town, a few minutes' walk from Cathedral Square, the Gediminas Tower hill and the start of Gedimino avenue. It is the best choice for visiting the Old Town, museums and restaurants in the centre.

## Location and access
- District: Senamiestis (Old Town)
- Approximate address: Vilniaus g., Vilnius (entrance from the Vilniaus street side)
- Walking distance: Cathedral Square about 5 minutes, Town Hall Square about 12 minutes.
- Public transport: several bus and trolleybus stops on Gedimino avenue and at the Cathedral.
- The Old Town has narrow one-way streets. Follow the "P – VilniusPark" signs from Gedimino avenue.

## Facilities
- 3 underground levels (-1 to -3)
- Height limit: 2.10 m
- 6 EV charging bays (22 kW, Type 2) on level -1
- 8 accessible spaces next to the elevators
- 2 elevators and stairs to street level
- 24/7 CCTV and staff on site during opening hours
- LPG vehicles are not allowed

## Good to know
- Popular on Friday and Saturday evenings and during events in the centre. Reserve ahead on weekends.
- Holders of a disability parking card park free for up to 2 hours.
