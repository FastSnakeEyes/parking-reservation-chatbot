# VilniusPark Station

## Overview
Open-air car park next to Vilnius railway station and the central bus station. It suits travellers catching a train or long-distance bus and people dropping off or picking up passengers.

## Location and access
- District: Naujamiestis
- Approximate address: Geležinkelio g., Vilnius (entrance from Geležinkelio street)
- Walking distance: railway station about 2 minutes, bus station about 4 minutes, Old Town Gate of Dawn about 10 minutes.
- The airport train departs from the railway station.

## Facilities
- Single level, open air, no height limit
- 5 accessible spaces near the station entrance
- Short-stay "Kiss & Ride" zone, free for the first 10 minutes (not reservable)
- Payment machines accept cards and cash
- 24/7 CCTV, lit at night

## Good to know
- Open around the clock, useful for early-morning and late-night trains.
- No EV chargers at this location.
