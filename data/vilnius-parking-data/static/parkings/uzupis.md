# VilniusPark Užupis

## Overview
Small courtyard car park in the artistic Užupis district, just across the Vilnia river from the Old Town. It is quiet and suits visits to Užupis cafés, galleries and the eastern part of the Old Town.

## Location and access
- District: Užupis
- Approximate address: Užupio g., Vilnius (entrance through the gate on the courtyard side)
- Walking distance: Užupis Angel square about 3 minutes, Bernardine Garden about 8 minutes.
- Narrow entrance. Wide vehicles (vans, large SUVs) should take care.

## Facilities
- Single level, open-air courtyard
- Height limit: 2.30 m (entrance arch)
- 2 accessible spaces
- Card payment only
- CCTV, gate closes outside opening hours

## Good to know
- Only 40 spaces in total, so it fills up quickly on weekends. Reservation is recommended.
- Closed on Sundays.
- Holders of a disability parking card park free for up to 2 hours.
- No EV chargers.
