# VilniusPark Business Center

## Overview
Multi-storey car park in the Šnipiškės business district, on the right bank of the Neris river near Konstitucijos avenue. It serves the surrounding office towers, the shopping centres on Konstitucijos avenue and the Municipality building.

## Location and access
- District: Šnipiškės
- Approximate address: Konstitucijos pr., Vilnius (entrance from the side street behind the office towers)
- Walking distance: Vilnius City Municipality about 5 minutes. The Old Town is across the White Bridge (Baltasis tiltas), about 20 minutes on foot.
- Public transport: many bus and trolleybus routes along Konstitucijos avenue.

## Facilities
- 5 levels (ground + 4)
- Height limit: 2.20 m
- 12 EV charging bays (11 kW AC) and 2 fast chargers (50 kW DC) on the ground level
- 10 accessible spaces next to the elevators
- Bicycle parking at the ground level
- 24/7 CCTV

## Good to know
- Very busy on weekday mornings (08:00–10:00). Reserve ahead for business meetings.
- Closed on Sundays and some public holidays.
- Cheaper on weekends.
