# VilniusPark Airport Long-Stay

## Overview
Open-air long-stay car park near Vilnius International Airport (VNO). It is meant for travellers leaving their car for several days. It is not an official airport car park. It is operated by VilniusPark.

## Location and access
- District: Kirtimai
- Approximate address: Rodūnios kelias, Vilnius (entrance next to the airport access road)
- Walking distance: airport terminal about 7 minutes. A free shuttle runs every 15 minutes between 04:00 and 24:00.
- The airport train connects the airport with Vilnius railway station.

## Facilities
- Single level, fenced, open air, no height limit
- 8 EV charging bays (22 kW, Type 2)
- 10 accessible spaces next to the shuttle stop
- Payment machines accept cards and cash
- 24/7 security staff, CCTV and barrier access

## Good to know
- Reservations up to 30 days long are possible here.
- The daily maximum applies to every started 24 hours, which makes long stays cheaper than hourly parking at the terminal.
- Reserve early around school holidays and Christmas.
