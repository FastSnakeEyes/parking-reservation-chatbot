# How to Reserve a Parking Space

## Reservation steps
1. **Choose a car park**: tell the assistant which VilniusPark car park you want, or describe where you are going and it will suggest one.
2. **Choose date and time**: give the arrival date and time and the departure date and time. Reservations are made in 30-minute steps.
3. **Check availability and price**: the assistant checks whether reservable spaces are free for the whole period and tells you the expected price.
4. **Give your details**: name, surname, car registration plate number, and a phone number or email for the confirmation.
5. **Administrator review**: your request is sent to a VilniusPark administrator, who approves or rejects it, usually within 15 minutes during support hours.
6. **Confirmation**: once approved, you receive a confirmation with a reservation number. The barrier recognises your plate number automatically on arrival.

## Reservation limits
- Minimum reservation length: 1 hour.
- Maximum reservation length: 14 days (Airport Long-Stay: 30 days).
- Reservations can be made up to 30 days in advance.
- One reservation covers one vehicle. Several vehicles need separate reservations.

## Arrival and departure
- You can enter up to 30 minutes before the reserved start time at no extra charge.
- The space is held for 60 minutes after the reserved start time. After that, the reservation may be released.
- If you stay longer than reserved, the extra time is charged at the standard hourly rate at exit.
- You cannot enter or leave a car park while it is closed, even with a reservation. Check the opening hours for your dates.

## Changes and cancellations
- Free cancellation up to 2 hours before the reserved start time.
- Later cancellations and no-shows are charged for the first hour of the reservation.
- To change the time or the plate number, ask the assistant. A change is a new request and goes through administrator review again.

## Why a reservation can be rejected
- No reservable spaces left for the requested period.
- The car park is closed during part of the requested period.
- The vehicle exceeds the car park's height limit.
- Missing or invalid details (e.g. an incomplete plate number).
